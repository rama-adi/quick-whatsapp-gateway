This guide gets you to a signed-in account. The gateway is the service that
holds the WhatsApp connection and serves the API. The dashboard is the web app
where you sign in and manage things.

## Open your dashboard

Use the dashboard URL provided by the person hosting your gateway. You also need
the public API URL when calling the gateway from your own code. The dashboard
shows it when you create an API key and on each session's overview.

## Create your account

Open the dashboard, select **Create one** below the sign-in form, and sign up
with an email and password. If registration is closed, the sign-in page asks you
to contact your gateway operator, who can create an account for you. New accounts receive ordinary user access and a personal organization.

After you sign in, the dashboard opens on **Home**. Its **Get started**
checklist walks you through the next steps. See [Home](/docs/guides/dashboard#home).

If you forget your password, the gateway cannot send a reset email. Ask your
gateway operator to set a new password, then change it from your
[account settings](/docs/guides/dashboard#your-account). The same page lets you
turn on two-factor authentication.

## Your personal organization

Every resource in the gateway, such as a WhatsApp number, an API key, or a
webhook, belongs to an **organization**, not to you directly. An organization is
a container that owns resources and can have several members. When you sign up,
the dashboard creates a **personal organization** for you and makes you its
owner.

For solo use you do not need to think about this. Every number and key you
create goes into your personal organization. Organizations matter once you want
to share a connection or keep separate projects apart. See
[organizations](/docs/guides/organizations).
